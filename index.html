How it works

Safety first

Lithium-ion cells can overheat, vent, or catch fire if shorted, overcharged, or damaged. This kit is for people comfortable working with batteries.

  • Never charge a cell that is swollen, leaking, or physically damaged.
  • Never leave cells charging unattended.
  • Work on a non-flammable surface, away from anything combustible.
  • Watch the full safety section of the tutorial before you start.
  • Battery cells are not included. You are responsible for safe use.
